VERSION 0.5.82
Base: 0.5.81
Changes: Removed the non-atmospheric grey Arctic "lens" that could appear over polar ocean even with all weather/atmosphere visibility disabled. The culprit was a second shader-local deep-cold phase guard in surface.glsl, separate from the physical cryosphere display.
Root cause: deepColdIce was a smooth 0..1 function across roughly 258.15..271.35 K and was then mixed directly into ocean colour. A broad polar temperature gradient therefore became a broad semi-transparent grey/white disc. This bypassed the 0.5.81 binary cryosphere map entirely, so hardening the cubemap could not remove it.
Fix: the emergency deep-cold closure is now a binary phase decision only below 258.15 K (-15 C). It remains solely to close sub-grid bays that the coarse Weather Core can miss at extreme cold. Near the ordinary freezing range the physical sea-ice field remains authoritative.
Sea-ice display: seaIcePhys and the deep-cold fallback now combine as binary coverage. Ice colour texture changes the material colour only; it no longer changes optical opacity. A covered ocean sample receives the ice surface colour directly instead of mix(ocean,ice,fraction).
Physical intent: sea-ice concentration represents area occupied by opaque floes and open leads, not translucency. The existing 0.5.81 cryosphere-edge display continues to spatially resolve partial physical concentration into opaque ice/open water patches.
Regression: cryosphere-hard-edge.test.js now also guards against a smooth deepColdIce temperature lens, shoreBiasedSea*iceMicro opacity, and any non-opaque covered ocean sample. Older biome/phase tests were updated semantically so they no longer require the removed translucent path.
Validation: confirm visible stamp v0.5.82. Disable every atmospheric visibility toggle and inspect a polar ocean. There must be no broad grey/fog-like disc. Genuine sea ice may occupy a large Arctic basin, but it must read as a solid surface/pack-ice region with exposed water where the coverage mask is open.
Snapshots: snapshot/0.5.78 through snapshot/0.5.82 are retained as parallel release checkpoints.
Retained: 0.5.81 hard cryosphere cubemap edge and +15 C cap trim; 0.5.80 gradual/irreversible volatile loss; 0.5.79 uncapped hot equilibrium; 0.5.78 phase/biology gating; 0.5.77 deep-freeze sub-grid closure.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
