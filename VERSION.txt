VERSION 0.5.165
Base: 0.5.164 current-T_a random-world correction.
Changes: fix the random-world orbital solver and bias generated worlds warmer.
  - 0.5.164 had the binary-search direction reversed: a cold Weather Core probe moved the orbit farther from the star, making already-cold worlds colder and commonly producing T_a around -60 °C.
  - Cold probes now move inward (smaller AU); hot probes move outward (larger AU).
  - Random-world target T_a is now +14 to +24 °C, inside the intended roughly +10 to +25 °C habitable range, with a +10 to +27 °C acceptance guard.
  - The regression test uses a monotonic synthetic climate and fails if a cold world is moved outward again.
  - Manual worlds and the underlying climate/weather physics remain unchanged; only the Random generator's orbital solve is corrected.
Regression: Random must converge toward a warm current Weather Core T_a and a cold probe must decrease orbital distance.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
