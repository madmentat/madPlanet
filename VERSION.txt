VERSION 0.5.57
Base: 0.5.56
Changes: Physical diurnal cycle. Weather Core local radiative energy is no longer permanently daily-mean: js/diurnal-cycle.js supplies a body-fixed physical sun that rotates around the resolved planetary spin axis using planetPhysics().rotationHours and core.simSeconds. 0.5.57 is intentionally equinox-only, so solar declination is zero and axial tilt does not yet create seasons. The user-facing/render uSunDir remains visual-only and cannot rewrite climate. local-energy integrates each fixed 300 s step at the midpoint solar phase; night-side cells receive zero direct shortwave, daytime cells receive instantaneous S*cos(SZA), while the area-weighted sphere mean remains approximately S/4 and therefore does not invent extra global energy. Persistent solarZenithCos/daylightFactor/localSolarTimeHours diagnostics expose the physical day/night state. Slow and fast rotators therefore develop different thermal amplitudes naturally: long nights permit stronger surface cooling and humidity increase, while daylight reheats the surface. Because the diurnal module executes immediately after local-energy and before pressure/wind/H2O/condensation/cloud-radiative/fog modules, the same fixed tick can propagate day/night temperature changes into pressure gradients, evaporation/condensation, cloud forcing and the 0.5.56 physical fog model. Per-cell hot loops allocate no helper vectors; only fixed Float32Array state is written. No diurnal work runs on requestAnimationFrame.
Roadmap note: next stage is 0.5.58 seasons / axial-tilt solar declination, building on the physical diurnal sun without coupling climate to renderer light controls.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
