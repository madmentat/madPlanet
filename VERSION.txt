VERSION 0.5.63
Base: 0.5.62
Changes: Aurora visibility and magnetic-geometry repair. The aurora remains a separate cheap additive WebGL pass and still requires atmosphere, magnetic field and solar activity, with strong optical suppression on the illuminated side. The main visibility defect was a colour-space mismatch introduced by the separate-pass architecture: the planet pass had already been tone-mapped and gamma-encoded, while auroral emission was added afterward as raw linear values, making physically valid dim curtains several times darker than intended. shaders/aurora-pass.glsl now applies a compact display-space compression and gamma conversion before additive compositing. The Kp response is also softened so ordinary active conditions are readable, strong activity widens the visible oval while keeping its nominal centre in the existing 75..59.5 degree magnetic-latitude range, and the broad sector noise keeps a small physical floor so it cannot randomly erase the whole oval. High-activity secondary arcs, folds and red/violet components are slightly strengthened without returning the old perfect-ring or techno-grid look. A tilted magnetic dipole is now treated as body-fixed and rotated around the spin axis with the planet instead of hanging motionless in inertial space while the surface turns underneath it. Regression coverage now checks the separate-pass display transform, widened Kp response, non-vanishing sector floor, body-fixed magnetic-axis rotation and inclusion of that rotation module in both shell and PowerShell builds.
Roadmap note: visually validate 0.5.63 on the nightside at default Earth-like settings and at Kp 8-9. The default should show broken green curtains/arcs near the magnetic oval when viewed from a favorable angle; maximum activity should expand and brighten the auroral region, add occasional red/violet structure and remain strongly subdued on the dayside. After that, return to long-run Weather Core/H2O normalization stability work unless another visible regression is found.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
