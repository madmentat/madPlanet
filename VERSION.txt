VERSION 0.5.59
Base: 0.5.58
Changes: Ocean mixed-layer heat storage / SST. New js/ocean-thermal.js separates the authoritative physical surface into a fast land-skin reservoir and a slow ocean mixed-layer reservoir instead of letting all cells share one globally blended heat capacity. The module consumes the already-resolved clear+cloud netRadiation once per fixed Weather Core tick and integrates landSurfaceTemp with 1.6e7 J m^-2 K^-1 while seaSurfaceTemp uses seawater rho*cp with a dynamic 8..85 m mixed-layer depth (35 m calm baseline, deepened by strong wind/deep convection). core.surfaceTemp is then republished as the local land/SST blend from surfaceWaterFraction, so existing evaporation and physical-fog code automatically see SST over ocean without new masks or duplicated water logic. The intermediate legacy local-energy surfaceTemp tendency is not accumulated as a second heat source; the two thermal reservoirs become authoritative after the radiative chain. Neighbouring wet cells exchange only heat through a conservative SST diffusion step on the existing H2O cubed-sphere edges; mixing scales as K*dt/L^2 with physical edge length, so draft/desktop grid resolutions do not get arbitrarily different ocean heat-spreading rates. This is deliberately thermal diffusion rather than a fake ocean-current model and it never changes H2O, pressure, wind or cloud condensate. New diagnostics expose mean land skin / SST, mixed-layer depth and land-ocean temperature contrast. 0.5.57 diurnal forcing and 0.5.58 tilt seasons now acquire real thermal memory and land-ocean contrast.
Roadmap note: next stage is 0.5.60 snow / sea-ice / surface ice physics, using the new land/SST reservoirs and seasonal cycle rather than the older global ice proxy alone.
