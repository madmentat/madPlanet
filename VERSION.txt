VERSION 0.5.65
Base: 0.5.64
Changes: Weather/visibility repair driven by deployed visual validation. The Details toggle was not merely changing render quality: Weather Core resolution depended on state.draft, so switching Details changed the grid from 32 to 48 and weatherCoreEnsure() destroyed/recreated the complete persistent atmosphere. Details is now renderer-only; desktop weather remains 48 cubed-sphere cells per face and mobile remains 32, preserving storms, fronts, fog and accumulated simulation state across the toggle. Lightning is fully decoupled from detail-dependent lower-cloud alpha: cloud alpha now controls only an irregular noisy/elliptical illumination field around a physical strike, while the direct channel remains visible independently of one-vs-three cloud volume samples. Maximum glow is stronger, ordinary mixed-phase cumulonimbus thresholds are softened without allowing shallow/warm or clear-sky strikes, and individual bolts receive deterministic random rotation, wide size/width/wobble variation and optional branches so adjacent strikes no longer form identical vertical copies. The screenshot trigger remains whole-visible-hemisphere and is resynchronized with the stronger renderer coefficients. Earth-like fog formation is tightened from generic humid-air support to genuinely near-saturated near-ground condensation; formation is slower, dissipation faster, maximum depth/optical density are capped, and neighbour smoothing is reduced from two 24% passes to one 12% pass so local fog banks do not spread into a Venus-like blanket. Aurora visibility is strengthened on the nightside: Kp response widens the irregular oval substantially at high activity, increases emission and twilight readability, integrates the thin auroral volume with more samples and retains explicit display-space conversion without the old 0.72 post-gamma suppression.
Validation note: check a temperate +21 C Earth-like world for at least several model hours; ordinary RH around the high-80% range must leave most terrain clear while genuine saturated calm regions may develop local fog. Toggle Details repeatedly while thunderstorms exist: weather diagnostics/ticks and lightning must persist. At maximum storm controls, strikes should vary in angle/size/branching and visibly light surrounding cloud masses with irregular patches rather than circular halos. For aurora, view the nightside or terminator at high Kp/magnetic field; daylight remains intentionally suppressive.
Roadmap note: after visual validation of 0.5.65, return to the long-run H2O-normalization stability investigation if the atmosphere no longer fogs over and weather survives render-quality changes.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
