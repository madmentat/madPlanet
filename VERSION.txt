VERSION 0.5.158
Base: 0.5.157 analytic vector rivers.
Changes: mobile-correct chord sampling and a cheaper channel loop.
  - The chord data samplers are declared highp: a default-precision sampler2D returns fp16 texels on mobile GPUs, which snapped unit-sphere endpoints to ~0.001 rad and collapsed every chord index above 2048 onto its even neighbour, so trunks rendered as dashed sausages while early-indexed feeders stayed intact.
  - The bin lists are de-indexed on the CPU (each entry carries its chord record), so the shader loop does two direct fetches per chord instead of a dependent index read plus two; the loop cap is 32 with 48 bins per face, and a fragment already inside a channel stops early.
  - The sub-grid meander warp uses two tangent-plane FBM samples instead of six volumetric ones; the riparian halo around a vector channel is narrowed to about 2.6 half-widths.
Regression: guards require highp chord samplers, direct chord reads from the de-indexed list, four transferable buffers and the retired index texture absent.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
