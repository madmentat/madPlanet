VERSION 0.5.164
Base: 0.5.163 weather telemetry and low-cloud visibility hotfix.
Changes: make city-ready Random target the actual current T_a shown by Weather Core.
  - The old generator solved climateModel()'s equilibrium temperature, while T_a is the current Weather Core surface mean and can be substantially colder after latitudinal thermal structure is applied.
  - A post-generation orbital solve now rebuilds Weather Core probes and adjusts distance against the actual area-weighted surface temperature.
  - Random worlds target T_a between +10 and +25 °C, with an acceptance band of +8 to +27 °C.
  - The correction is restricted to the Random generator; manual worlds, weather physics, rivers and terrain are unchanged.
Regression: Random must optimize CURRENT Weather Core T_a rather than only the climate attractor.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
