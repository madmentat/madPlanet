VERSION 0.5.70
Base: 0.5.69
Changes: Polar-ice visual reconstruction pass. The persistent Weather Core still owns the continuous physical fractions for snow, land ice and sea ice, including latent heat, albedo feedback and water-budget closure. Rendering no longer interprets those physical fractions literally as translucent white paint. The cryosphere GPU map is reconstructed at 2x the physical grid resolution (64x64 per face on mobile, 96x96 on desktop), bilinearly sampled from the coarse physics and passed through a narrow visual coverage transfer curve. A low-cost seamless spherical edge perturbation only shifts the threshold inside already transitional physical cells, giving continental ice sheets and pack ice a large-scale irregular natural outline without inventing ice or exposing cubed-sphere squares.
Edge behaviour: dense land ice now becomes visually opaque and reads as a coherent Antarctic-style sheet; thin/seasonal marginal coverage can remain broken. Sea ice receives a separate slightly softer transfer curve so pack-ice margins stay irregular rather than looking like a white cloud laid over the ocean. Texture filtering remains LINEAR to avoid pixel blocks, but the source reconstruction is denser before thresholding.
Temporal fix: the old cryosphere GPU crossfade lasted about 0.9 seconds while Weather Core publishes once per second, leaving moving ice margins almost permanently in a semi-transparent intermediate state. The display interpolation window is now about 0.36 seconds (bounded 0.12..0.56 s), so ice still moves without harsh popping but spends most of each weather tick as a crisp surface boundary.
Performance note: physical Weather Core resolution is unchanged. Only the small fixed-tick RGBA8 render cubemap is 2x per axis; this adds roughly 4x cryosphere texture pixels but no per-frame physics work and no additional texture unit. Regression coverage locks the visual/physical separation, 2x reconstruction, seamless threshold breakup and shortened crossfade.
Retained: 0.5.69 cold-stress biomes/inland-water freezing, 0.5.68 broken auroral curtains and 0.5.67 hydrology-shaped vegetation remain unchanged.
Validation note: inspect a mature cold world with clouds visually hidden. A polar continent should now have a readable, fairly sharp irregular ice-sheet edge rather than a broad foggy fade. Seasonal snow outside the dense sheet may remain patchy; sea-ice margins should be crisp but more fragmented than continental ice. If the whole cap is still diffuse after sufficient model time, the next target is physical accumulation geometry rather than display filtering.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
