VERSION 0.5.124
Base: 0.5.123
Changes: Make orbital shape readable in the HUD and recalibrate polar/thermal diagnostics exposed by real-world testing.
  - keep physical Kepler eccentricity separate from display shape; climate, periapsis/apoapsis, orbital timing and 1/r^2 forcing continue to use the physical e
  - broaden seeded physical eccentricity to e=0.006..0.220 with only a mild low-e bias so Random produces more varied but still planetary orbits
  - add a shared visual eccentricity mapping e_vis=0.36..0.62 for the mini-map and main-scene navigation orbit; ordinary e values that are mathematically almost circular now render as clearly readable ellipses
  - both orbit displays label the physical e and explicitly identify the drawn orbit as a schematic; the main-scene planet remains pinned to screen centre and the sun remains at the displayed focus
  - recalibrate Thermal Imager colour allocation: most palette resolution now covers -100..+60 C instead of being compressed by the +1200 C lava tail
  - climate-range contours tighten from 20 C to 10 C while volcanic temperatures keep a separate compressed hot tail up to +1200 C
  - legend now exposes <=-100, -50, 0, +50 and +1200 C reference points so Antarctic-class cold is visually distinguishable from merely freezing terrain
  - add polar continentality physics: high-latitude land cells surrounded by land receive an additional bounded cold-trap term, strongest over snowy/icy continental interiors and in polar darkness
  - continental cooling is geography-aware, fades toward coasts/open ocean, is disabled on genuinely hot worlds, and extends the existing surface-energy budget rather than recolouring the renderer
  - a broad icy polar continent can now run tens of kelvin colder than a maritime polar coast even at the same latitude, complementing the existing plateau lapse-rate and sea-ice skin physics
Regression: orbit-eccentricity.test.js now separates physical and visual eccentricity contracts; thermal-celsius.test.js guards climate-focused palette allocation; polar-continentality.test.js validates interior-vs-coastal polar cooling and build order.
Snapshot: 0.5.123 remains the rollback point before HUD eccentricity amplification and stronger continental polar thermodynamics.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
