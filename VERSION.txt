VERSION 0.5.100
Base: 0.5.99
Changes: Straight knife-cut seams through rivers/land (e.g. seed 58532301) persisted after ONB/island/cryo fixes. Root cause: land biomes used soil moisture + surface temperature from the low-res fog/weather cubemap (channels B/A). Cubemap face edges project as great circles and appear as hard linear cuts across continuous FBM rivers and continents — independent of Tectonics.
Fix:
  fog.glsl — physicalFogSample averages B/A over a few-degree tetrahedron (R/G fog stays single-tap)
  surface.glsl — mix ≥45% continuous FBM into soil moisture and surface temperature so cube faces cannot own albedo
Snapshots: snapshot/0.5.99-pre-cubemap-biome is the rollback point.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
