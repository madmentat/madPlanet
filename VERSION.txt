VERSION 0.5.48
Base: 0.5.47
Changes: local Weather Core vertical stability/cloud layers v1 - the resolved surface/air temperature contrast, local relative humidity, pressure scale height and orographic ascent now diagnose a compact atmospheric-column stability state without introducing a fake 3-D atmosphere. A dew-point/LCL estimate sets physical cloud-base height, bulk environmental lapse is compared with dry/moist parcel lapse to diagnose stable/neutral/unstable/deep-convective columns, and cloud-top depth grows with buoyancy and resolved lift. Existing physical cloudWaterState is partitioned conservatively into low/mid/high condensate masses by geometric overlap with scale-height bands, with per-cell cloudLowMass + cloudMidMass + cloudHighMass exactly equal to the authoritative bulk condensate. The layer pass is diagnostic/partition-only: it does not create/delete H2O, alter temperatures, or touch GLSL cloud morphology; visual coupling remains scheduled for 0.5.53.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
