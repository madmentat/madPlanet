VERSION 0.5.159
Base: 0.5.158 vector rivers with highp chord samplers.
Changes: continuous channels and natural junctions on the synoptic grid.
  - Interior drainage-graph nodes are relaxed toward the midpoint of their main upstream and downstream nodes (two bounded passes); sources, ocean receivers and coastal nodes stay put, and every painter reads node positions through one accessor, so the D8 staircase of right angles becomes a gently curved trunk that tributaries meet exactly.
  - The last edge of a visual tributary takes the receiver's downstream cell as its far spline handle, so mouths curve into the trunk's flow direction instead of meeting it at a right angle.
  - Bins are 64 per cubemap face with a 0.010 rad margin, which keeps crowded bins under the 32-chord shader cap that previously dropped weaker chords and left gaps.
  - Orbit views skip the sub-pixel meander warp; ",rivvec=0" in the URL hash keeps the raster corridor path for on-device frame-cost comparison.
Regression: guards require node relaxation before painting, the acute-mouth handle, the diagnostic hash switch and bounded node displacement on a synthetic staircase.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
