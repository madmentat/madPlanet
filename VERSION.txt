VERSION 0.5.109
Base: 0.5.108
Changes: Make polar ice caps visibly non-circular at planetary scale instead of only roughening the same latitude ring.
  - preserve the restored 0.5.107 classic planet morphology and the 0.5.108 opaque/binary cryosphere edge; no translucent polar fringe returns
  - add a body-fixed, seed-dependent global polar warp with several low-order sectors (1/2/3/5-lobed components) and a hard maximum displacement of 0.18 rad (~10.3 degrees)
  - north and south poles use independent seeded sector phases, so they do not become mirrored copies of one another
  - displace the physical cryosphere sampling direction along the local meridian before thresholding; even a physically sharp latitude snow line therefore develops continent-scale bays, eccentric sides, peninsulas and ice tongues
  - keep the global warp restricted to high latitudes and near-zero at lower latitudes so ordinary alpine snow and mid-latitude terrain are not dragged around
  - retain the seamless 3-D broad/regional/local/fine edge field only for shoreline-scale breakup after the large cap geometry has already been displaced
  - keep visible ice strictly binary/opaque, NEAREST-filtered and without temporal alpha crossfade; the old grey Arctic mould cannot return through this path
  - keep the +15 C ~10% transitional-cap trim from 0.5.81/0.5.108
Regression: strengthen cryosphere-hard-edge.test.js with a behavioural orbital-scale guard: around a representative 67-degree latitude the seed-90636862 cap sampling latitude must span more than 12 degrees, preventing another visually negligible "edge-noise-only" fix.
Snapshot: snapshot/0.5.108 remains the rollback point before this deliberately large global cap deformation. Create snapshot/0.5.109 only after final CI passes.
