VERSION 0.5.154
Base: 0.5.153
Changes: Natural river geometry at every scale; rivers no longer vanish at orbit.
  - js/river-gpu.js (model 14): after Chaikin the polyline is refined by seeded three-level midpoint displacement (amplitude proportional to segment length), so a smoothed lattice staircase becomes a meandering river instead of a straight canal; painted node positions are remembered so a tributary snaps to the drawn stem and stops there; the last node of a tributary is pulled downstream along its receiver so confluences are acute, not perpendicular comb teeth; stems are painted before visual branches
  - shaders/surface.glsl: the drainage map is read with five taps over the pixel footprint when a pixel spans more than one texel (max of taps), so a one-texel channel stays a continuous one-pixel line from continent to orbit; the far-distance fade is gone; frozen channels render as pale translucent ice threads
  - uRiverTexel uniform (river-render.js) carries the texel size
Regression: river-physics and river-visual-tributaries tests updated.
Snapshot: 0.5.153 remains the rollback point before the fractal river geometry.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
