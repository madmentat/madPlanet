VERSION 0.5.88
Base: 0.5.87
Changes: The 0.5.87 A/B test falsified the stronger pairCompetitive gate as the visible stripe root cause: raising exp(-130*(di^2+dj^2)) to exp(-280*(di^2+dj^2)) produced no material visual change. A stale surface-only compatibility macro was then found to be bypassing the corrected 0.5.86 plate-boundary field entirely.
Root cause: shaders/surface-artifact-prelude.glsl still redefined gSeamNear as mix(0.145,0.052,ss(0.010,0.095,mount)). During preprocessing of surface.glsl this silently replaced float seamNearCenter = gSeamNear with a mount-derived pseudo seam. Therefore the real nearest/second-nearest weighted-Voronoi boundary computed by terrain.glsl never reached surface shading. The fake mount contours were subsequently reused by both volcanic-arc pigmentation and the optional Plates diagnostic, explaining smooth lines that could cross inside one displayed plate and strengthen as Tectonics changed mount.
Fix: removed the gSeamNear macro and its matching postlude undef. surface.glsl now snapshots the actual terrain global before its finite-difference normal samples overwrite terrain diagnostics. The cryosphere texture-AA wrapper remains unchanged.
Isolation: retain pairCompetitive=280 and the existing global normal gain float bmp=(0.03+0.095*uTect) for this release. If residual stripes remain after the seam-routing fix, the next isolated suspect is that global Tectonics-dependent normal amplification rather than plate-pair geometry.
Regression: added tectonic-surface-seam-routing.test.js and wired it into run-all.sh. It forbids any surface prelude/postlude shadowing of gSeamNear, requires terrain's nearest/second-nearest seam publication, and verifies that volcanism and the Plates overlay consume the real centre-point seam snapshot.
Validation: reproduce the marked seed/area and sweep Tectonics 0% -> 100%. With Plates enabled every diagnostic line must coincide with an actual colour boundary; no line may cross a single-colour plate. With Plates hidden, volcanic activity may form broken clusters near genuine margins but must not reveal smooth mount-contour arcs. Compare against snapshot/0.5.87.
Retained: 0.5.87 stronger pair gate; 0.5.86 true nearest/second-nearest plate diagnostics; prior performance, cryosphere, extreme-temperature, cloud, lightning and aurora fixes.
Snapshots: snapshot/0.5.87 is the rollback point before this seam-routing fix. Create snapshot/0.5.88 only after the final develop head passes CI.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
