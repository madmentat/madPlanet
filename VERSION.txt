VERSION 0.5.78
Base: 0.5.77
Changes: Extreme-temperature surface phase closure, two-sided biological temperature limits, residual tectonic/volcanic stripe repair, and stronger Android viewport locking.
Thermal transport: the Weather Core surface-temperature GPU channel no longer clips every value to 180..380 K. A piecewise 8-bit codec now reserves 0..0.05 for 80..180 K, 0.05..0.90 for the high-precision 180..380 K climate range, and 0.90..1 for 380..1000 K. This keeps essentially the old precision around Earth-like conditions while allowing the renderer to distinguish -160 C from merely cold weather and +627 C from an ordinary hot climate.
Biomes: visible terrestrial biology now has both a cold and a hot envelope. Cold support still rises through roughly 268..285 K; heat stress now removes living green cover through roughly 308..333 K. Above that range the surface transitions to sterile mineral/rock palettes regardless of stale soil moisture or procedural hydrology. Riparian vegetation, dense forest colouring and city lights all obey the same thermal habitability envelope.
Liquid water: ocean, lakes, rivers and water specular now obey physical phase limits instead of geometry alone. Near ordinary climates the renderer uses an atmosphere-pressure-dependent boiling estimate; above the water critical region the liquid gate closes unconditionally. Superheated ocean geometry is rendered as a dry/mineral basin rather than blue water. Inland liquid water uses the same hot-phase gate.
Deep freeze: 0.5.77's physical sea-ice skin remains authoritative on the Weather Core grid, but the renderer now also applies a local deep-cold closure across procedural coastlines and inland hydrology. This fixes sub-grid cases where a coarse physics cell was classified as land while the high-resolution terrain shader contained a narrow bay. Below the deep-freeze transition such bays cannot leak blue liquid water merely because the cubemap and procedural coastline disagree by one cell.
Tectonic artifacts: shadeSurface now snapshots gSeamNear/gSeamConv/gPlateTint immediately after the centre terrain sample. Finite-difference terrain samples used for normals can no longer overwrite the tectonic diagnostics later consumed by volcanism and the optional plate overlay. Volcanic arcs are also broken into broad irregular provinces before the small vent mask, so volcanism no longer paints a continuous dark ink line along an otherwise valid tectonic seam.
Touch: canvas gestures now additionally lock root/body horizontal overscroll and restore the gesture-start viewport scroll position while a captured touch/pen pointer is active. Legacy touchstart/touchmove cancellation supplements Pointer Events for Android Chromium builds that still leak a small visual/layout viewport pan. Panel and slider gestures remain on their separate DOM path.
Regression: new extreme-surface-phase.test.js guards the extended temperature codec, hot/cold biological gates, pressure/critical liquid-water shutdown, sub-grid deep-freeze closure, centre-point tectonic diagnostics and segmented volcanic arcs. biome-state.test.js now verifies -160 C and +627 C survive the codec instead of requiring the retired v2 180..380 K clamp. The full regression suite is green before this release bump.
Physics reference: ordinary water has a critical temperature near 647 K; above that region an Earth-like open liquid ocean is not a valid surface phase. The pressure-dependent boiling approximation below the critical region is intentionally compact rather than a complete steam-table solver.
Validation: confirm visible stamp v0.5.78. Recheck seed 40491874 at the same close tectonic view, at about -160 C and at about +627 C. Deep-freeze seas/bays and inland water should show ice rather than blue liquid; furnace worlds should contain no green vegetation or blue liquid surface. On Android, a one-finger planet drag must not shift the page/toolbars sideways. If a thin dark line remains, compare once with Vulkanism=0: a line that survives that toggle belongs to terrain/normal morphology rather than the volcanic display path addressed here.
Retained: 0.5.77 physical cold sea-skin, continuous tectonic seam field and PointerEvent ownership; 0.5.76 smooth dRef relief weights; 0.5.75 non-periodic mountain folds and dense ice display; 0.5.74 cubemap seam-aware cryosphere reconstruction; 0.5.73 slow persistent glacier formation.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
