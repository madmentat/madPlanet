VERSION 0.5.87
Base: 0.5.86
Changes: A/B test of Grok's residual tectonic ghost-arc hypothesis. Only the local pair-competition gate is strengthened; the existing tectonic normal/bump gain is intentionally left unchanged so visual results isolate one variable.
Relief gate: pairCompetitive changes from exp(-130*(di^2+dj^2)) to exp(-280*(di^2+dj^2)). Remote/non-competitive plate pairs therefore lose amplitude much faster before they can engrave mathematical bisectors into the terrain.
Bump gain: deliberately retained at float bmp = (0.03 + 0.095*uTect). Grok's proposed reduction to 0.055 is NOT included in this test release; if the stripes disappear, the pair gate is implicated rather than a general weakening of tectonic relief.
Regression: tectonic-cell-seams.test.js and visual-regressions.test.js require the stronger 280 gate. The visual suite also guards that the 0.095 tectonic bump coefficient remains unchanged for this A/B.
Validation: use seed 90636862, the same marked island, and sweep Tectonics 0% -> 100%, preferably with Plates visible for orientation. Long smooth arcs inside a single plate should be reduced or disappear without making genuine ridges globally flatter.
Retained: all 0.5.86 nearest/second-nearest plate diagnostic logic, continuous outer tectonic envelope, current surface bump strength, and prior performance/cryosphere/weather fixes.
Snapshots: snapshot/0.5.86 is the rollback point before this A/B. Create snapshot/0.5.87 only after the final develop head passes CI.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
