VERSION 0.5.160
Base: 0.5.159 analytic vector rivers.
Changes: river courses come from the rendered terrain, not from the synoptic grid.
  - terrain-bake.js links a terrain-only program (header + noise + terrain + terrain-bake.glsl) and reads six 256x256 (mobile 224x224) faces of sea-relative terrain height back as 24-bit fixed point, once per terrain signature (seed, sea, continents, tectonics, islands, plates).
  - river-drainage-fine.js fills depressions with Priority-Flood plus epsilon, routes steepest-descent D8 on the filled surface, accumulates runoff-weighted contributing area from the Weather Core's basin runoff, starts channels above 40 wet face-centre cells and publishes source-to-mouth chains (relaxed against the staircase, mouths trimmed to the shoreline) as chords for the analytic renderer, plus the raster corridor and depression lakes.
  - The Weather Core worker receives the height buffer with the tick request, builds the drainage off the main thread and forces a river refresh; the main thread builds locally only when no worker exists.
  - The synoptic river graph stays as the fallback for contexts without a terrain bake; rivers can no longer cross mountain belts, join as combs or form coast-to-coast canals, and every basin drains to exactly one outlet.
Regression: guards require the bake conventions, the transferred height buffer, fine ownership of the river bridge, monotonic drainage on a synthetic planet, lake filling of a closed pit, seam adjacency, chain consistency and a dry basin without channels.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
