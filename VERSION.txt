VERSION 0.5.84
Base: 0.5.83
Changes: Broke up the remaining mathematically smooth tectonic arcs/ribbons, reduced tectonic fragment cost, further softened tablet fixed-tick hitches, and let adaptive resolution react during close pinch/drag instead of freezing quality while FPS collapses.
Tectonic root cause: plate interaction still used the analytic weighted-Voronoi pair coordinate (dj-di)/|pj-pi| as the actual ridge/rift centreline. Global domain warp bent that geometry somewhat, but high Tectonics amplified the surviving spherical arcs into long dark/light ribbons. Convergent boundaries also carried a compulsory parallel trench and divergent boundaries were engraved as continuous negative grooves.
Tectonic geometry fix: after the plate pair and its local tangent boundary normal are known, the seam coordinate itself is displaced by the existing 3-D coarse/fine warp projected onto that pair normal. This costs no extra noise calls inside the O(N^2) pair loop and prevents the exact power-Voronoi arc from surviving into terrain. A non-periodic rupture field additionally fragments long belts. Convergence now produces broken uplift without an automatic parallel trench; divergent relief is much shallower and spatially broken.
Tectonic performance: the old positive-belt detail stack used roughly twelve secondary ridged/fbm octaves on top of the already expensive plate-pair search. It is replaced by a 3-octave ridge plus two independent single noise samples. tectonicBelt() is also skipped completely when both Tectonics and the plate diagnostic overlay are disabled.
Tablet cadence: touch/mobile Weather Core uses 28 cells per cube face on ordinary devices and 24 on low-memory devices instead of 32, reducing one fixed-step field from 6144 cells to 4704 or 3456 cells. The physical timestep remains deterministic and fixed.
Cryosphere cadence: the expensive 4x visual cryosphere reconstruction is no longer rebuilt every real second on mobile. It publishes a new visual target about every 2.4 s while the physical cryosphere continues ticking normally. Because the current cryosphere map is a scalar boundary field rather than optical alpha, the display edge can safely interpolate between publishes and remain opaque after the shader threshold.
Close-zoom FPS: 0.5.83 blocked dynamic-resolution changes while a pointer was down. That prevented adaptation exactly during pinch zoom, when the planet fills more pixels and fragment cost rises. 0.5.84 permits controlled degradation during interaction, with a longer interaction cooldown and recovery only after interaction stops. Mobile minimum internal scale is 0.68 normally or 0.60 on low-memory devices.
Telemetry: retained the FPS / mean temperature / spectral class / AU readout added in 0.5.83 so close-zoom and periodic stalls remain directly measurable on-device.
Regression: tectonic-morphology.test.js now guards pair-local seam warp, non-periodic rupture, removal of compulsory convergent trenches, shallow broken rifts and the cheaper mountain-detail stack. tectonic-cell-seams.test.js keeps the differentiable all-pair reference/continuous diagnostic field while requiring actual seam-coordinate warp. smooth-motion-ui.test.js guards 24/28-cell mobile Weather Core, slower cryosphere publishing/interpolation, interaction-time adaptive resolution and retained HUD.
Validation: confirm visible stamp v0.5.84. On a previously affected seed, compare Tectonics near minimum versus maximum while zoomed close: mountain systems should strengthen, but perfectly smooth globe-scale dark/light arcs should not emerge. Pinch into a surface view and watch FPS; quality may step down once under sustained overload instead of remaining pinned at a high render scale until fingers are released. Small periodic weather hitches should be reduced further.
Snapshots: snapshot/0.5.83 was created from the fully green 0.5.83 head 862347a4debc0a51acb798a86d131f8a70707ffd before starting this release. Older snapshot/0.5.78 through snapshot/0.5.82 remain retained.
Retained: 0.5.83 smooth opaque ice contour and live HUD; 0.5.82 no grey Arctic lens; 0.5.81 opaque cryosphere phase logic and +15 C cap trim; 0.5.80 gradual/irreversible volatile loss; 0.5.79 uncapped hot equilibrium; 0.5.78 phase/biology gating.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
