VERSION 0.5.180
Changes: terrain-routed rivers with inland headwaters and terminal water outlets.
  - Replaced noise-contour channels with a directed downhill drainage graph sampled from the rendered terrain.
  - Inland runoff accumulation selects headwaters; tributaries merge and carry water through dry downstream reaches.
  - Seas and lakes are terminal receivers, never river sources. Closed dry depressions are not breached uphill.
  - Analytic segment rendering keeps channels narrow independently of cubemap resolution; width grows with catchment supply.
  - No procedural channels are shown while the terrain sampler is unavailable or linking.
Validation: Node regression suite and GPU terrain/drainage/rendering probe.
Regression seeds: 8127344, 55807208, 5569959.
Compatibility: analytic rivers require WebGL2 and texture unit 8. The labelled compatibility planet renderer remains available.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
