VERSION 0.5.125
Base: 0.5.124
Changes: Restore physically truthful orbital geometry and make real mountain elevation visible in both climate and thermography.
  - remove the 0.5.124 HUD-only eccentricity exaggeration: mini-map and main-scene orbit now draw the same physical e used by Kepler timing, periapsis/apoapsis and 1/r^2 climate forcing
  - keep the main-scene guide at a stable screen-space size for usability, but no longer distort its ellipse shape or focus position; near-circular planetary orbits are allowed to look near-circular
  - retune seeded eccentricity for mature planetary systems to e=0.002..0.210 with a strong low-e bias (power 2.60): most worlds are close to circular while a Mercury-like tail remains possible
  - retain orbitDisplayEccentricity only as a compatibility API and make it an identity function so display e can never silently diverge from physical e again
  - fix the missing mountain term in physical surface temperature: the existing plate-derived orographicRoughness field is converted to resolved mountain relief and added to macroTerrain elevation before the 6 K/km lapse-rate budget
  - use the same 5.2 km effective relief scale already used by orographic lift, conservatively converted to mean ridge height; high mountain belts can now cool the actual Weather Core by tens of kelvin instead of being climate-flat decorations
  - raise the physical elevation ceiling from 5 to 9 km so terrestrial high mountains are representable without clipping
  - keep continental/polar inversion physics from 0.5.124; a +13 C mean Earth-like world can now physically resolve <-60 C exposed surfaces on high Antarctic-style interior mountains without a palette trick
  - Thermal Imager evaluates the exact rendered terrain once and adds only a bounded sub-grid peak residual (max about 2.4 km / 14.4 K) above the coarse Weather Core mountain belt, so individual ridges appear colder without double-counting a second global climate
  - thermal legend now reports live physical minima for the whole surface, north polar region, south polar region and resolved mountains, making it immediately obvious whether a suspicious colour is a palette issue or a climate-model issue
Regression: orbit-eccentricity.test.js forbids visual e amplification and checks the low-e mature-system distribution; polar-surface-thermodynamics.test.js verifies plate-derived mountain relief and a <-60 C high-polar mountain at +13 C global mean; thermal-celsius.test.js guards sub-grid mountain lapse thermography and live Celsius minima.
Snapshot: 0.5.124 remains the rollback point before restoring truthful HUD eccentricity and coupling resolved tectonic relief into surface temperature.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
