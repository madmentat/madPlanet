VERSION 0.5.162
Base: 0.5.161 terrain-driven rivers with review fixes.
Changes: continuous, curved and width-graded channels on dense networks.
  - Chord bins are 128 per cubemap face with a 0.008 rad margin and the shader visits up to 48 chords per bin; on a land-heavy world the 64-bin index overflowed the 32-chord cap in hundreds of bins and dropped the weakest chords, which read as rivers breaking near confluences.
  - Fine channels use their own width curve (0.00035 + 0.0030*s^1.8 + 0.001*ws^2 rad), about 8:1 between a threshold creek and an Amazon-class trunk instead of 2:1.
  - Chain relaxation runs three passes with a 0.6-cell bound and the sub-grid meander warp uses three octaves at 0.0046 rad, so the 40 km D8 staircase reads as a curve instead of a crowbar.
  - Basin wetness has a 0.30 floor (a dry basin needs ~3x the catchment, not infinite) and an unbootstrapped runoff memory counts as uniform wetness, so a fresh or loaded world grows rivers immediately instead of waiting 24 model days.
  - Worker mirrors publish immediately: the idle-slot river pacing was written for main-thread reconstruction and starved a device that never reports an idle frame.
Regression: guards require the finer bins, the fine width curve, the wetness floor and bootstrap fallback, and the immediate mirror publication.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
