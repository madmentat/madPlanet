VERSION 0.5.183
Changes: physically coherent curved rivers and no painted bank halos.
  - Smooth main stems and tributaries along a shared tangent field on the terrain drainage graph.
  - Rasterize two Hermite chords per terrain edge with the existing packed integer atlas (no extra GPU textures).
  - Remove independent shader jitter, zoom-driven screen-space stroke widening and dark floodplain outlines.
  - Use river widths in world coordinates; screen pixel size affects antialiasing only.
  - Narrow isolated headwaters gradually instead of thick abruptly capped channels.
  - Preserve one-outlet drainage topology, 14-bit cubemap endpoints and two candidate segments per atlas texel.
  - Refresh continuity tests against the curved rather than straight channel geometries.
Validation: full Node regressions, GLSL ES 300 shader compile, cube-seam atlas checks.
