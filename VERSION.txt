VERSION 0.5.44
Base: 0.5.43
Changes: local Weather Core condensation v1 - supersaturated vapor now condenses into a persistent physical cloud-water column in kg/m2, undersaturated air can re-evaporate existing condensate, and both phase changes conserve local H2O while applying bounded latent heating/cooling to the air column. Cloud condensate is advected conservatively by the same tangent wind field and cross-face edge graph as vapor. The global atmospheric H2O closure now preserves vapor plus cloud water together against the conserved 0.5.37 reservoir, preventing the old vapor-only normalization from creating water on top of condensate. Precipitation remains zero until 0.5.45.
