VERSION 0.5.80
Base: 0.5.79
Changes: Cryosphere display rewritten to remove milky Antarctic fringe, square/orthogonal ice artefacts and sine-plane striping; close-orbit water/atmosphere loss is gradual in-session and irreversible on moving the same world back outward; extreme Weather Core forcing is staged rather than rebuilt in one frame; dotted tectonic/volcanic pigment arcs are suppressed without removing tectonic relief.
Cryosphere root cause: 0.5.79 thresholded the coarse interpolated land-ice coverage against cryoGpuEdgeNoise(), and that field was a sum of sine planes. Close views therefore exposed both the piecewise cubemap interpolation geometry and the planar noise as polygonal cap outlines and perpendicular bands. Sea-ice concentration was also still mixed directly into colour, which produced the translucent white "mould" around cold continents.
Cryosphere display: physical land/snow and sea-ice fractions remain untouched. Display now treats them as spatial area coverage. A seamless 3-D hashed value-noise field evaluated from sphere direction decides which reconstructed sub-cells are opaque ice and which are exposed ground/water. It contains no Math.sin plane bands and no face-x/y geometry. Dense continental ice is solid. Partial sea-ice concentration becomes coherent floes and open leads instead of every pixel receiving fractional white alpha.
Surface stripe fix: the extremely thin dark/dotted arcs visible on smooth barren close-up worlds were consistent with volcanic pigment reading the exact tectonic seam distance. The relief itself remains tectonic, but surface colouring now sees only a broad orographic support during surface.glsl; the exact one-pixel seam is no longer used as an ink line. The macro is scoped strictly around surface.glsl and is undefined immediately afterwards.
Extreme climate: the 0.5.79 removal of the accidental 900 K / 626.85 °C cap remains. Very high-flux rocky worlds can still reach the physically hotter equilibrium range up to the 2200 K safety bound.
Volatile history: the age/flux/escape-velocity retention formula is retained as an asymptotic target, not an instantaneous multiplier. Within one seed/runtime, H/He, water inventory and heavy atmosphere decay toward that target over several-to-tens of seconds and can only decrease. Moving the same world outward stops additional escape but does not resurrect already escaped atmosphere or water. A new random seed starts a new equilibrium world/history.
Water behaviour: ordinary water-budget evaporation/condensation is again allowed to be visible as a process. The conserved H₂O inventory itself erodes more slowly under extreme irradiation, while phase transfer continues through the existing water-budget relaxation. A brief hot excursion can therefore vaporise water and later re-condense retained water; prolonged severe exposure permanently removes part of the total inventory.
Weather response: 0.5.79's one-frame Weather Core replacement is retired. Major forcing (>70 K, >2.5× flux or >4× pressure) opens a ~15 s transition window. About once per real second the existing core's surface temperature, air temperature and pressure are nudged toward the new target while fronts/storms/cloud state remain live. This is fast enough to avoid the old minute-long stale Earth, but slow enough to show boiling/cooling instead of an instant texture cut.
Snapshots: snapshot/0.5.78 and snapshot/0.5.79 have been restored as parallel immutable checkpoints before this release line.
Regression: extreme-orbit-regressions.test.js now guards non-instant severe loss, irreversible outward return, hot climate above 900 K, staged thermal transition without replacing Weather Core, 3-D non-sine cryosphere coverage, opaque land ice / floe-style sea ice, and scoped tectonic pigment suppression.
Validation: confirm visible stamp v0.5.80. On a cold world, inspect Antarctica both close and far while rotating: there should be no fixed perpendicular band texture, no square/polygonal white edge and no broad translucent white wash. Partial sea ice should read as patchy/floe-like coverage. On a smooth dry/hot world, rotate and zoom: the old dotted great-circle-like dark arcs should no longer trace plate seams. For a temperate world dragged inward, ocean/atmosphere loss should develop over seconds rather than one frame; after moving back outward, escaped atmosphere should remain missing.
Retained: 0.5.79 uncapped hot equilibrium and volatile physics; 0.5.78 thermal biology/liquid-water phase gating and Android viewport locking; 0.5.77 deep-freeze water closure; 0.5.74 seam-aware cubemap reconstruction.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
