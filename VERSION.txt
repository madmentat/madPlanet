VERSION 0.5.69
Base: 0.5.68
Changes: Cold-world surface consistency pass. Physical Weather Core surface temperature now gates living biome colour independently of water availability, so wet river/lake corridors stop remaining summer-green through deep frost. Coarse temperature still acts only as an envelope: a reused fine ecological field perturbs the thermal threshold by a few kelvin so low-resolution cubed-sphere cells cannot print square cold boundaries onto the surface. Cold stress moves vegetation toward winter-grey/tundra colours before snow arrives, and moist deep-freeze ground gains a pale frost veil while authoritative snow/land ice still comes from the persistent cryosphere texture. Procedural inland rivers and lakes now have a local freezing phase derived from Weather Core temperature; frozen inland water changes to ice colour and loses liquid-water specular highlights instead of remaining dark blue at sub-freezing temperatures. Ocean/sea ice remains owned by the physical cryosphere model.
Diagnostics: the Planet panel now shows current Weather Core surface temperature for the equator (±15°), temperate latitudes (30–60°), polar latitudes (>75°), and the current global min…max. This distinguishes an instantaneous global climate target from a persistent atmosphere/surface field that is still thermally adjusting. The legacy snowAlt control no longer pretends to be a metre-valued physical snow line; it is labelled as orographic cooling, matching its remaining shader role.
Validation note: on a world with a headline mean near -46 °C, the latitude diagnostics may initially show a transient warmer equator if the climate has only just collapsed, but green riparian cover should already be strongly suppressed wherever the local physical surface is below the biological range. Inland lakes/rivers should freeze visually around 270–274 K. Persistent snow/ice coverage should continue to grow with model time rather than being painted instantaneously from the headline mean temperature.
Retained: 0.5.68 broken auroral curtains and 0.5.67 hydrology-shaped vegetation remain unchanged.
Roadmap note: next inspect the multi-ridge tectonic comb seen in seed 2662238 and decide whether to turn it into a hierarchical fold belt (one dominant range plus weaker offset secondary ranges) instead of parallel equal-width grooves. Continue persistent biome recovery/seasonality/fire state and long-run H2O-normalization stability after cold-world validation.
