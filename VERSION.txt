VERSION 0.5.81
Base: 0.5.80
Changes: Polar/continental ice display is now strictly opaque at the resolved surface edge; the remaining translucent white fringe is removed. The visible temperate polar cap is calibrated about 10% smaller around a +15 °C global mean without deleting the dense inner ice core.
Root cause: even after 0.5.80 converted fractional physical ice into spatial patches, three optical interpolation paths could still manufacture grey/white "mould": cryoGpuVisualCoverage retained a feather around each threshold, the cryosphere cubemap used LINEAR texture filtering, and the renderer cross-faded old/new hard ice masks between Weather Core updates. Those are sensible for clouds, but wrong for a solid ice-sheet boundary.
Hard edge: physical snow/land-ice and sea-ice fractions remain continuous in Weather Core for mass, latent heat, albedo and water-budget physics. The display transfer is now binary: a resolved surface sample is either opaque ice or exposed land/water. There is no fractional display feather.
GPU sampling: the 5x cryosphere render cubemap now uses NEAREST sampling so adjacent binary ice texels are not blended into a translucent optical rim. The short old/new cryosphere display crossfade is disabled; the newest resolved geography is shown directly at each weather update. Physics itself is not made discontinuous.
Sea ice: partial physical concentration is resolved into opaque floes and open leads. Concentrations below 15% are not painted as a coherent polar fringe, matching the common operational sea-ice-edge convention while leaving the underlying physical fraction untouched.
Temperate cap calibration: around a 288.15 K (+15 °C) mean climate, transitional (non-dense) cryosphere coverage is multiplied by 0.90 before the spatial coverage decision. Dense continental ice (>=0.70 physical coverage) is exempt, so the change trims the outer visible cap by roughly 10% rather than hollowing the interior. This visual calibration fades away over roughly ±18 K so deep-freeze and extreme-hot regimes are not globally retuned.
Regression: cryosphere-hard-edge.test.js guards strict 0/1 visible coverage, no feather transfer, the 15% sparse-sea-ice cutoff, NEAREST cubemap sampling, no temporal display crossfade, and the +15 °C ~10% edge trim. Existing cryosphere seam/physics tests remain active.
Validation: confirm visible stamp v0.5.81. With atmospheric visibility disabled, polar ice must end at a solid, crisp boundary: no broad translucent white halo around the cap. At about +15 °C mean climate the cap should be visibly around 10% smaller than the prior calibration while preserving its dense interior. Close/far views and rotation should not reveal a grey interpolation fringe.
Snapshots: snapshot/0.5.78, snapshot/0.5.79, snapshot/0.5.80 and snapshot/0.5.81 are retained as parallel release checkpoints.
Retained: 0.5.80 gradual in-session volatile escape and irreversible outward return; 0.5.79 uncapped hot equilibrium; 0.5.78 phase/biology gating; 0.5.77 deep-freeze surface closure; 0.5.74 seam-aware cubemap reconstruction.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
