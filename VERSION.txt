VERSION 0.5.46
Base: 0.5.45
Changes: local Weather Core orographic lift/rain-shadow v1 - the existing tectonic orographic resistance field is differentiated on the cubed-sphere neighbour stencil into a physical terrain-slope proxy and projected onto the resolved tangent wind to estimate upslope/down-slope vertical motion. Upslope flow applies bounded dry-adiabatic cooling before the existing saturation/condensation stage; descending flow warms the air and raises its saturation deficit, so windward cloud/precipitation and leeward rain shadow emerge through the already mass-conserving H2O phase chain rather than by deleting moisture. Latent heat remains owned exclusively by condensation.js, and the dry orographic temperature tendency is area-mean neutral each tick so relief cannot create a global heat drift. No new procedural weather morphology or terrain CPU rebuild is introduced.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
