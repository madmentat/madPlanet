VERSION 0.5.66
Base: 0.5.65
Changes: Biome-state and surface-inspection pass. The Weather panel now exposes a compact three-button cloud visibility strip for lower/middle/upper decks. These controls are explicitly renderer-only: they keep the existing lowOn/midOn/highOn hash compatibility and render/shadow behavior but never create, reset or step Weather Core, so users can hide any cloud deck to inspect terrain and then reveal the still-evolving physical weather again. Dynamic land appearance now consumes real Weather Core surface state without allocating another fragment texture unit: the renderer-unused B/A channels of the existing double-buffered fog cubemap carry physical soil wetness and surface temperature, while R/G remain fog optical depth/depth. Static climate still defines which biome is plausible, but current soil moisture and heat now determine drought stress. Mild drought yellows vegetation toward straw, stronger drought exposes dull dry soil, and only severe hot dryness approaches sand/red-rock colours; dieback is spatially broken rather than a hard climate contour. Most importantly, the physical cryosphere is now composited after vegetation, drought, beaches and rivers/lakes, so a frozen grassland/forest becomes genuinely white instead of later biome detail repainting snow green. Active volcanic vents are applied after this final snow layer and may locally break through it. New regressions verify renderer-only cloud controls, Weather Core soil/temperature packing, drought inputs/palette stages and final cryosphere ordering.
Validation note: hide all three cloud layers and inspect a temperate world while changing its thermal/water regime. Frozen regions should progressively become white wherever Weather Core snow/land-ice coverage exists; vegetation detail must not show through established snow. In warm drying regions, green should first desaturate/yellow, then break into straw/dry-soil patches, with sandy/red-rock tones reserved for the hottest strongest drought. Restoring hidden cloud layers must reveal the current weather rather than freshly initialized clouds.
Roadmap note: continue biome work after visual validation by separating long-lived biome potential (climate/terrain) from additional dynamic ecological state such as vegetation recovery time, fire/burn scars and seasonality. The long-run global H2O-normalization investigation remains the main atmospheric architecture task once the new surface-state visuals are confirmed.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
