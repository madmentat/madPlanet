VERSION 0.5.73
Base: 0.5.72
Changes: Polar cryosphere morphology + landed-water freeze repair. With all atmospheric rendering disabled, 0.5.72 exposed that the remaining white polar feature was genuinely surface cryosphere: its edge still inherited too much of the coarse Weather Core cubemap and its dense interior read as a smooth white decal. The render-only cryosphere reconstruction is now 3x the physical grid instead of 2x. Its former pair of broad sinusoidal edge waves is replaced by four unrelated seamless spherical scales, producing large embayments, tongues/peninsulas and smaller edge roughness without any latitude-shaped mask or cube-face seams. Transitional land ice uses a much narrower visual transfer curve so fractional physical coverage becomes geographic patches rather than translucent milk. Dense land ice remains nearly opaque but retains a few percent of underlying terrain lighting/texture; sea ice still becomes exactly opaque at full physical concentration.
Physics fix: mildly sub-freezing landed rain/runoff no longer converts directly into persistent landIceWater in one five-minute tick. That old branch was limited only by the full land-skin heat capacity and could freeze tens of kg/m2 almost instantly, allowing a warm planet with a marginally cold pole to accumulate an implausibly massive glacier. Landed liquid now freezes at a hard maximum of 18 kg/m2 per model day (scaled down further near 0 C) into the seasonal surfaceSnowWater store. Only a sustained deep snowpack under sufficiently cold conditions can subsequently compact through the existing slow snow->land-ice path. Sea-ice latent-heat physics is unchanged.
Performance: Weather Core resolution remains 32 mobile / 48 desktop. Only the small RGBA8 display cubemap rises from 64/96 to 96/144 per face; uploads still occur on the slow weather tick, never at render FPS. No new physical grid, texture unit or per-frame CPU simulation was added.
Reference target: real Antarctica is a continent surrounded by ocean, while seasonal sea ice fringes it and advances/retreats separately. Ice shelves are floating glacier-fed extensions along selected coasts, not a uniform circular polar decal. 0.5.73 therefore keeps physical land ice and sea ice distinct and focuses only on making the land-ice edge read as geography rather than fog.
Validation note: reload the same warm seed so the 0.5.72 mean-temperature correction also starts from a fresh Weather Core. Hide all atmospheric effects. A marginal polar snow/ice field should now have a sharper, broken outline with bays/tongues and much less cubemap stepping; a warm +20…+30 C world should also accumulate persistent land ice much more slowly because transient frozen runoff stays seasonal. If the WHITE boundary becomes geographic but the underlying polar LAND coastline itself remains an implausibly round disk, the next isolated target is coastline morphology in terrain.glsl rather than another cryosphere mask.
Retained: 0.5.72 independent atmospheric diagnostic toggles and mean-neutral temperature bootstrap, 0.5.71 shore-first lake/ocean freeze morphology, 0.5.69 cold-stress biomes/temperature diagnostics, 0.5.68 broken auroral curtains and 0.5.67 hydrology-shaped vegetation remain intact.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
