VERSION 0.5.121
Base: 0.5.120
Changes: Replace the unstable main-scene orbit projection with a fixed-scale Elite-style navigation HUD orbit.
  - remove the singular radius solve that derived ellipse size from projected star/planet separation and divided by the projected minor axis
  - main-scene orbit now uses a fixed screen-space radius, so rotating the camera cannot shrink it into a tiny ring or expand it across several screens
  - camera zoom is intentionally excluded from navigation-orbit scale; the overlay is a readable HUD instrument rather than astronomical geometry rendered to scale
  - the current star-to-planet radial vector is forced into the displayed orbital plane, while the plane remains as close as possible to the physical ecliptic normal derived from axial tilt
  - the current planet is the t=0 orbit point and is pinned exactly to screen centre every frame, eliminating the previous vertical drift above/below the planet
  - when the viewer looks nearly along the star direction the projected orbit naturally approaches an edge-on line through the planet instead of collapsing to a displaced miniature ellipse
  - orbit styling is rebuilt as a soft amber navigation line: faint glow, dim dashed far side, crisp near side, subtle quarter-orbit pips and a compact current-body crossing marker
  - the normalized radius is bounded for phone and desktop viewports so the complete schematic remains comfortably readable without taking over the scene
  - display state remains local-only and does not alter the world seed, hash, climate, orbit physics or camera controls
Regression: rubric-orbit-ui.test.js now guards fixed HUD radius, radial-plane consistency, exact planet-centre crossing and bans the old singular projected-star radius formula.
Snapshot: 0.5.120 remains the rollback point before the stabilized Elite-style main-scene orbit.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
