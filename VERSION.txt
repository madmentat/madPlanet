VERSION 0.5.68
Base: 0.5.67
Changes: Aurora de-eye pass after deployed 0.5.67 confirmed that the previous visibility repair had overcompensated. The current default already keeps auroraOn=true, so visibility must no longer be guaranteed by forcing a luminous minimum around the complete magnetic oval. The 0.5.63..0.5.67 non-zero sector floor is removed entirely. Kp now widens a broad physical precipitation zone while the visible emission remains several much thinner independently broken curtain ribbons inside that zone; high activity adds extra equatorward/poleward filaments instead of thickening one annulus. Large magnetic-local-time gaps can therefore become genuinely dark. Face-on polar views are modestly suppressed while tangential paths toward the limb are strengthened, giving orbital views brighter upright curtains without painting the whole polar disc. Fine fold frequencies are raised, altitude colour layers are narrowed, the auroral shell returns to roughly the pre-overcompensation 90..430 km scale, and display power is reduced from the 0.5.65 fluorescent calibration while remaining plainly visible on the nightside. Regression coverage now explicitly forbids sector floors/fat 8.8-degree single bands and locks auroraOn=true as the default so a future accidentally-disabled switch is not 'fixed' by brightness inflation.
Validation note: at default/moderate Kp, a polar camera may still reveal the physical auroral oval but it should read as broken narrow arcs with substantial dark gaps, not a thick green eye. Rotate toward an oblique limb view: active arcs should brighten and appear more curtain-like, with occasional red high-altitude and violet low-altitude structure. Clouds beneath the aurora may be illuminated visually by additive overlap, but the auroral geometry itself should stay filamentary rather than becoming dirty broad cloud-shaped blobs. At Kp 8-9 the occupied latitude region should expand by adding/separating ribbons, not by turning into one solid torus.
Roadmap note: 0.5.67 hydrology-shaped vegetation is retained; its first deployed visual check suggests riparian greening is moving in the right direction. After aurora validation, continue biome ecology (persistent recovery/seasonality/fire state) and the long-run H2O-normalization stability investigation.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
