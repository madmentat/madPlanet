VERSION 0.5.92
Base: 0.5.91
Changes: Pause button that freezes all simulation processes for clean screenshots.
Placement opposite the hamburger (rub-toggle):
  - landscape / wide: top-right
  - portrait / narrow: bottom-left
While paused: simulation clock, planet/cloud rotation, weather physics, derived slider relaxation and camera inertia are frozen. Rendering continues so the current frame remains visible. Resume restores the clock without a jump. Spacebar toggles pause when focus is not in an input.
Snapshots: snapshot/0.5.91-pre-pause is the rollback point.
Retained: stabilised tangent basis (0.5.91), localTectSupport bump gate (0.5.90) and prior tectonic fixes.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
