VERSION 0.5.112
Base: 0.5.111
Changes: Make polar ice geographic instead of a latitude circle after every Random world.
  - new js/ocean-heat-transport.js: persistent surface heat forcing resolved from the actual cubed-sphere geography — warm inflow only along an open equatorward ocean pathway, warm east side / cold west side of each basin, maritime spill-over onto nearby coasts, and thinner greenhouse column over high terrain
  - the forcing is a zero-mean redistribution inside every latitude band and globally, so climateModel().T and the zonal bootstrap stay the headline; its steady-state anomaly Q/lambda is applied at Weather Core creation and maintained by the same flux every fixed tick
  - sea-ice skin, sea-ice growth, snow line and albedo feedback now read a temperature that varies by basin, coast and plateau, so the cap forms tongues, bays and an eccentric outline from the first frame
  - cryosphere-edge-display.js: retire the 0.5.109 display-side meridional warp that displaced ice off its own coastlines; sample the physical field at the true direction, keep only shoreline-scale edge noise
Regression: add tests/ocean-heat-transport.test.js (closure, basin-side asymmetry, landlocked polar sea, elevation, maritime coast, bootstrap and rebuild continuity); update cryosphere tests for the retired warp.
Snapshot: 0.5.111 remains the rollback point before geographic polar ice.
