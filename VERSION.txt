VERSION 0.5.64
Base: 0.5.63
Changes: Lightning follow-up and screenshot-trigger repair on top of the 0.5.63 aurora visibility fix. The lightning screenshot trigger had drifted away from the renderer: it still used the pre-0.5.62 linear storm-rate/activity scales, retained an extremely high legacy threshold, and multiplied flashes by a smooth camera-facing factor that effectively watched only the central portion of the visible disc. js/screenshot-trigger.js now mirrors the current nonlinear renderer equations, uses a calibrated physical-flash threshold, and treats visibility as a binary body-to-world hemisphere test. Any storm centre on the hemisphere facing the viewer, including the mathematical limb, is eligible regardless of camera distance; the hidden hemisphere remains excluded. Lightning abundance is increased without returning procedural clear-sky strikes: mixed-phase/deep-convective physics still creates the electrical potential, but flash cadence now responds sub-linearly to low/moderate nonzero potential so genuine ordinary thunderstorms do not wait unrealistically long between visible flashes. The renderer selection floor is reduced from 0.010 to 0.006, while maximum storm-rate/activity controls are strengthened and kept exactly synchronized with the screenshot trigger. New regressions cover near-limb and distance-independent capture, back-hemisphere rejection, renderer/trigger coefficient synchronization, moderate-storm cadence and the continued prohibition on lightning from shallow warm clouds. GitHub Actions CI is restored as an automatic build/test check only; it does not participate in runtime rendering or application behavior.
Aurora note: retain the 0.5.63 separate-pass colour-space, Kp-width and body-fixed magnetic-axis repairs unchanged. Validate the current build on the nightside before making any further aurora brightness changes, because the user report that aurora was effectively absent predates or overlaps that fresh fix.
Roadmap note: visually validate three things before returning to long-run H2O normalization work: (1) default Earth-like weather should show occasional but clearly noticeable lightning, (2) maximum storm activity/rate/glow should produce frequent obvious flashes in several real storm regions, and (3) an armed lightning screenshot trigger must fire for a visible flash anywhere from disc centre to limb at both close and distant camera positions. Also verify 0.5.63 aurora on the nightside at default activity and Kp 8-9.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
