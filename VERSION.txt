VERSION 0.5.71
Base: 0.5.70
Changes: Shore-first freeze morphology pass. Procedural inland lakes no longer use one uniform temperature mask across the whole basin. The existing lake height/noise field is reused as a cheap basin-depth proxy: shallow margins receive the warmer freeze threshold while deeper interiors require several additional kelvin of cooling, so early ice should establish around the shore and advance inward instead of appearing first as an isolated central white patch. Rivers use a separate slightly colder freeze response because moving water can remain open longer than sheltered lake shallows. Liquid and frozen inland fractions are now tracked separately in the shader, so only the genuinely liquid part keeps the water specular highlight.
Ocean behaviour: physical sea-ice concentration remains authoritative and still comes from Weather Core SST/latent-heat state. Within partially frozen physical cells only, visual coverage is redistributed modestly toward shallow coastal bathymetry. The bias is multiplied by 4*f*(1-f), making it exactly zero at both f=0 and f=1: no sea ice can appear where physics says none, and fully frozen cells remain fully frozen. This is a morphology correction for the advancing pack-ice edge, not a decorative replacement for cryosphere physics.
Performance: no new texture, Weather Core field, FBM field or per-tick physical grid was added. Lake depth reuses lakeN and ocean shore preference reuses the already calculated depth value, so the change is essentially arithmetic in the existing surface pass.
Retained: 0.5.70 crisp Antarctic-style cryosphere reconstruction, 0.5.69 cold-stress biomes and temperature diagnostics, 0.5.68 broken auroral curtains and 0.5.67 hydrology-shaped vegetation remain unchanged.
Validation note: during gradual cooling, inspect a large inland lake before complete freeze-up. A broken pale rim / marginal sheets should appear before the deeper centre closes. At strong sustained frost the complete lake may still become ice-covered. Ocean pack ice should similarly favour shallow coastal water only while physical concentration is partial; wind/current dynamics are not yet represented by this visual redistribution and remain a future physical refinement.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
