VERSION 0.5.56
Base: 0.5.55
Changes: Physical near-surface fog. The old procedural fog heuristics (subtropical inversion latitude belt, generic coastal mask and terminator fog) are retired. New js/physical-fog.js derives persistent fogState/fogOpticalDepth/fogDepthM from resolved Weather Core near-surface conditions: relative humidity, dew-point deficit / LCL, stable or surface-cooled air, local ocean/surface/soil wetness, precipitation, resolved wind and deep-convective mixing. Formation and dissipation have separate finite response times (about 45 and 32 simulated minutes before forcing modifiers), so a fog bank cannot appear or disappear in one fixed tick. Strong dry/warm/windy/turbulent conditions act as dispersers, while saturated calm stable air gradually forms fog. Existing fog state is advected by resolved wind over the already precomputed H2O edge graph; this transports only the fog visual/weather state and does not create/destroy H2O or modify pressure/wind. New js/fog-gpu.js publishes fog optical depth/depth/formation/dissipation through two RGBA8 cubemaps on texture units 5/6. Like the stabilized cloud bridge, each Weather Core target is double-buffered and render-time interpolated with uFogBlend; no texture upload occurs at render FPS. shaders/fog.glsl now samples only this body-fixed physical field, with procedural FBM limited to weak internal density texture that is multiplied by physical optical depth and cannot create fog where Weather Core has none. Diagnostics report fog cover/max state and mean optical depth/depth. The 0.5.54 cloud morphology/inertia/temporal interpolation and 0.5.55 cloud radiative feedback are preserved unchanged.
Roadmap note: next stage is 0.5.57 diurnal cycle, after validating fog formation/dissipation and advection visually across humid, dry, calm and windy worlds.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
