VERSION 0.5.89
Base: 0.5.88
Changes: Residual smooth curved stripes remained after the 0.5.87 pairCompetition experiment and the 0.5.88 real-seam routing fix. This release combines the two strongest remaining ideas instead of weakening all terrain globally.
Physical relief support: keep pairCompetitive=280, then additionally multiply belt.x / belt.z / leeOut by a broad real-boundary support seamGate = 1 - ss(0.075,0.240,gSeamNear). This adopts Grok's useful 0.5.87b insight that tectonic height should not survive deep inside a plate, but deliberately avoids Grok's much narrower exp(-28*gSeamNear), which would squeeze mountain systems into boundary ribbons.
Normal-path fix: the Tectonics slider no longer amplifies finite-difference normals for every land pixel on the planet. During surface.glsl only, uTect is remapped through local support from actual mount/ridge relief or proximity to the real centre-point seam. Genuine tectonic terrain keeps the existing 0.095 full-strength bump response; deep plate interiors fall back to the neutral 0.03 base normal gain instead of magnifying unrelated base-terrain gradients into arcs.
Seam integrity: the 0.5.88 removal of the fake mount-derived gSeamNear macro remains intact. Plate diagnostics and volcanism continue to consume the real nearest/second-nearest boundary snapshot.
Regression: added tectonic-interior-artifacts.test.js. It guards the broad real-seam physical gate, forbids Grok's overly narrow exp(-28*gSeamNear) form, verifies the surface-only local normal support and ensures the uTect remap is scoped away after surface.glsl.
Validation: use the previously problematic seed/area, zoom in and sweep Tectonics 0% -> 100%. Distant smooth arcs should no longer become stronger merely because Tectonics is raised. Real mountain systems and actual plate-margin structure should still strengthen. With Plates visible, diagnostic lines must still coincide with colour boundaries.
Retained: 0.5.88 real seam routing, 0.5.87 stronger pairCompetition, 0.5.86 nearest/second-nearest diagnostics, and all prior performance/cryosphere/weather/extreme-temperature fixes.
Snapshots: snapshot/0.5.88 is the rollback point before this combined fix. Create snapshot/0.5.89 only after the final develop head passes CI.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
