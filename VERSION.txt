VERSION 0.5.39
Base: 0.5.38
Changes: stellar-climate-weather coupling + Weather Core v1 - spectral class now sets a main-sequence mass/luminosity baseline while the luminosity slider is an independent multiplier; orbital distance spans 0.01..1000 AU around the preserved 1 AU pivot; old v6 and earlier links migrate their absolute luminosity and AU into v7 so existing worlds keep the same physical stellar forcing. Calculated temperature is no longer clamped to the legacy +97 C visual ceiling, so extreme greenhouse climates remove mountain snow and sea ice correctly; derived cloud, wind, convection, storm and snow-line controls relax toward climate-dependent targets; real multi-atmosphere steam inventories remain visibly thick instead of saturating the old renderer proxy. A deterministic persistent cubed-sphere CPU Weather Core stores surface/air temperature, pressure, humidity, cloud water, tangent wind and precipitation fields at 32x32x6 draft/mobile or 48x48x6 normal resolution, using a fixed five-minute model step on a one-second real-time tick independent of render FPS and with no hidden-tab catch-up. Hotfix: volcanism again affects climate through derived CO2 greenhouse and SO2 aerosol burdens layered on top of user gas inventories, without renormalizing or overwriting the user's atmosphere settings.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
