VERSION 0.5.58
Base: 0.5.57
Changes: Physical seasons / axial-tilt solar declination. The 0.5.57 body-fixed physical sun now changes declination over a circular orbit instead of remaining permanently at equinox. Axial tilt comes from planetPhysics().axialTiltDeg; orbital distance and stellar mass come from orbitDistanceAU() and starPhysics().M, with the year length following Kepler's third law P = 365.2568983 d * sqrt(a^3/Mstar). Orbital phase advances only with core.simSeconds and is deterministic from the world seed. The user's renderer uSunDir/light controls remain visual-only. No seasonal temperature multiplier is introduced: tilt changes the actual solar direction, so local solar elevation and day length modify instantaneous shortwave forcing through the existing diurnal/local-energy path; pressure, wind, H2O, clouds, cloud radiative feedback and physical fog respond downstream. New persistent dayLengthHours plus orbitalPeriodSec/seasonOrbitPhase/solarDeclinationDeg/axialTiltDeg diagnostics expose the seasonal state. At zero obliquity declination stays zero all year; at nonzero tilt the solstices reach +/- tilt, high latitudes naturally enter polar day/night, and the instantaneous sphere-mean max(cos SZA,0) remains approximately 1/4 so no global energy is invented. Orbit eccentricity is intentionally absent because it is not yet a BASE input; 0.5.58 therefore models tilt-driven seasons on circular orbits only. Per-cell hot loops allocate no helper vectors and all seasonal work remains on the fixed Weather Core clock.
Roadmap note: next stage is 0.5.59 ocean heat / SST, so the new diurnal and seasonal forcing can acquire realistic thermal memory and land-ocean contrast.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
