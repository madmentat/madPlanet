VERSION 0.5.113
Base: 0.5.112
Changes: Stop polar caps from growing without limit and make the two hemispheres differ.
  - new js/atmospheric-heat-transport.js: energy-balance diffusion of surface heat along the cubed-sphere edge graph (D = 0.55 W m^-2 K^-1 on Earth, scaled by the atmospheric column cp p / g and planet radius); conservative and stability-capped per edge
  - polar cells previously sat at -80..-150 W m^-2 net radiation with nothing to balance it, so every cap kept growing at ~0.8 K/day regardless of the mean climate; poleward transport now pays that deficit as it does on a real planet
  - seasonal bootstrap at Weather Core creation: the steady anomaly of the current declination relative to the annual-mean daily insolation is applied (land ~0.75, ocean ~0.12 response), so the winter cap is larger than the summer cap from the first frame
Regression: add tests/atmospheric-heat-transport.test.js (conservation, pole warming, airless and 90-bar limits, seasonal asymmetry, wrapper integration).
Snapshot: 0.5.112 remains the rollback point before atmospheric heat transport.
