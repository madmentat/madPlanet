VERSION 0.5.75
Base: 0.5.74
Changes: Polar ice display sharpening + tectonic stripe removal. The remaining close-up polar cap in 0.5.74 was still too soft/polygonal on mobile because the render-only cryosphere cubemap had only three display samples per Weather Core cell and dense land ice deliberately varied its coverage below 1.0. The display reconstruction is now 5x the unchanged physical grid (160 per face on mobile, 240 desktop), adds two finer seamless spherical edge scales, makes dense physical land ice exactly opaque, and shortens temporal crossfade so a sharp moving margin does not spend most of the one-second weather interval as translucent grey. Weather Core remains 32/48 and no new texture unit or per-frame CPU simulation is added.
Artifact fix: the smooth dark bands still visible on exposed mountain belts were not cryosphere seams. terrain.glsl itself generated collision folds with a periodic cos(distance_to_plate_seam*78 + ...), which necessarily stamped evenly spaced ribbon-like ridges and valleys across large regions. That periodic term is removed. Collision belts retain their tectonic envelope but internal relief now comes from two unrelated non-periodic spherical ridge/noise fields, so mountain chains can remain elongated without forming ruler-smooth parallel stripes.
Performance: the denser cryosphere map is still rebuilt only on the slow weather tick. Mobile storage for the RGBA display cubemap remains well under 1 MB; CPU work increases only for this once-per-second reconstruction, while fragment shader cost and Weather Core physics resolution are unchanged. The tectonic replacement uses existing FBM/ridged primitives and removes the trigonometric distance stripe generator.
Reference target: real Antarctic ice is often smooth at continental scale but not visually fog-like; wind-shaped sastrugi, crevasses, shelf edges and exposed nunataks provide surface structure, while large-scale topography remains coherent. The display mask therefore stays solid where physical ice is dense and reserves fine morphology for the geographic edge/terrain texture rather than encoding texture as transparency.
Validation note: verify the visible stamp says v0.5.75. With atmospheric effects hidden, inspect the same close polar view: the cap edge should have smaller/finer geographic steps and a dense interior should read as solid snow/ice rather than a translucent patch. On exposed tectonic terrain, the former smooth black parallel bands should be absent; remaining elongated ridges should be irregular, branching and interrupted.
Retained: 0.5.74 seam-aware cubemap reconstruction and burial of subglacial hydrology, 0.5.73 slower persistent glacier formation, 0.5.72 atmospheric diagnostic toggles/mean-neutral climate bootstrap, 0.5.71 shore-first water freezing, 0.5.69 cold-stress biomes, 0.5.68 auroral curtain breakup and 0.5.67 hydrology-shaped vegetation remain intact.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
