VERSION 0.5.136
Base: 0.5.135
Changes: Remove fractional degrees from the compact T_a/T_f thermometer.
  - show T_a and T_f as whole Celsius degrees in the top-left live telemetry instead of switching between integer and fractional representations
  - keep the fixed 104 px right-aligned value column and tabular numeric glyphs from 0.5.135, so signs and changing digit counts do not reflow the block
  - retain full floating-point temperature values internally; only the compact headline presentation is rounded, so climate physics and detailed diagnostics lose no precision
  - keep T_a sourced from the current area-weighted Weather Core surface field and T_f sourced from the equilibrium climate forecast
  - leave detailed Weather/Planet diagnostics free to show decimals where they are useful for debugging and quantitative inspection
Regression: thermal-celsius.test.js now requires whole-degree headline formatting and forbids the old magnitude-dependent fractional precision rule.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
