VERSION 0.5.132
Base: 0.5.131
Changes: Remove the tilted chessboard artifact from physical rivers without rolling back runoff-driven drainage.
  - diagnose the artifact correctly as exposed numerical-grid geometry: 0.5.131 allowed almost every wet Weather Core catchment cell to become a visible channel, then connected those cells through a four-neighbour cubed-sphere stencil
  - add a dedicated eight-neighbour D8-like river routing stencil across cube-face seams, giving Priority-Flood and steepest-descent routing diagonal choices instead of forcing E/W/N/S Manhattan drainage
  - keep a single Weather Core cell as a hillslope/catchment element rather than automatically treating its local runoff as a river; visible channel initiation now requires accumulated contributing area plus accumulated discharge
  - express the channel-initiation thresholds relative to current cell area and climate-supported reference runoff, so desktop/mobile grid resolution and planet radius do not resurrect a dense one-cell river mesh
  - preserve conservative discharge, hydraulic width/depth/velocity and stream-power calculations from 0.5.131; the refinement changes the resolved channel-support mask, not the water budget
  - replace straight cell-centre display links with deterministic sub-cell curved segments whose endpoints remain exactly connected at confluences; this hides residual lattice direction while retaining the physical drainage graph
  - narrow the GPU splat radius so channel corridors read as rivers rather than broad edges of computation cells; lakes retain separate depression/water support
  - keep all routing/raster work on Weather Core fixed ticks and continue temporal cubemap blending, with no hydrology or texture generation on render FPS
Regression: river-physics.test.js now requires eight-neighbour routing, contributing-area channel initiation, rejection of a one-cell pseudo-river, survival of a multi-cell trunk, curved graph rasterization and correct build order.
Snapshot: 0.5.131 remains the rollback point for the first runoff-driven network; 0.5.130 is the pre-physical-river rollback.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
