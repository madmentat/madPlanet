VERSION 0.5.91
Base: 0.5.90
Changes: Thin discontinuous lines, interrupted colour changes and classic "triangle without hypotenuse" artefacts were still visible even at Tectonics=0. They originated from the finite-difference tangent basis
  tg = normalize(cross(n0, abs(n0.y)<0.99 ? upY : upX))
which degenerates and flips orientation near the poles and at the hard switch threshold. After normalize() the numerical noise becomes visible as sphere-discretisation stripes.
Fix: replace with a continuously oriented non-degenerate frame
  if (abs(n0.z) < 0.999) tg = normalize(vec3(n0.y, -n0.x, 0.0));
  else                   tg = normalize(vec3(0.0, n0.z, -n0.y));
  bt = cross(n0, tg);
This keeps the basis well-defined everywhere on the sphere.
Validation: same seed / camera as the reported screenshots (e.g. 82765821), Tectonics both 0 and 1. Thin interrupted lines and triangular geometry artefacts should disappear; real tectonic relief and plate boundaries remain.
Snapshots: snapshot/0.5.90-pre-basis-fix is the rollback point.
Retained: localTectSupport bump gate from 0.5.90 and all prior tectonic fixes.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
