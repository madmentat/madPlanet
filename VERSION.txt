VERSION 0.5.74
Base: 0.5.73
Changes: Cryosphere cubemap seam repair + subglacial hydrology visual burial. The long dark/bright curved stripes visible across polar ice were not geological features: the render-only cryosphere upsampler performed bilinear interpolation by clamping all four source taps to the current cubemap face. Near a face edge that repeated the last row/column instead of reading the physically adjacent face, so cube boundaries projected onto the sphere as giant great-circle arcs. Bilinear corner taps may now leave the source face: the extended face coordinate is converted to a real 3-D sphere direction and mapped back onto the canonical Weather Core cubed sphere before reading the source cell. This keeps reconstruction continuous across all six faces without blurring the actual geographic ice edge or changing physical cryosphere state.
Hydrology display fix: the bright closed loops and occasional dark threads seen inside dense polar ice were procedural inland lakes/rivers leaking through the partially opaque snow layer. Hydrology still contributes to soil/ecology and can physically exist below ice, but its visible water/ice masks now fade rapidly with authoritative land-cryosphere coverage and with displayed snow. Dense ice sheets therefore bury the procedural river/lake network instead of showing a false white/dark map on top of the glacier.
Performance: no new texture unit, physical field, per-frame CPU loop or higher Weather Core resolution. Seam-aware sampling only replaces four cheap CPU source lookups while rebuilding the already-existing 3x cryosphere display cubemap on the slow weather tick. Fragment cost is effectively unchanged; hydrology burial is two smoothstep-style arithmetic gates in the existing surface shader.
Diagnostics: the user screenshots that exposed this problem still displayed v0.5.72 in the upper-left corner. 0.5.73 had not been deployed/cached there yet, but the cubemap clamp bug also existed in 0.5.73 and is fixed here. After deployment verify that the visible version stamp says v0.5.74 before comparing the same polar view.
Validation note: hide all atmospheric effects and inspect a large polar sheet at close zoom. There should be no long single-pixel/dotted arcs following cube-face geometry. Rivers and lake outlines should disappear beneath substantial land ice while remaining visible on exposed or lightly snow-covered ground. The actual outer ice margin can remain irregular from the 0.5.73 geographic morphology pass.
Retained: 0.5.73 slower persistent land-glacier formation and 3x geographic polar reconstruction, 0.5.72 atmospheric diagnostic toggles/mean-neutral climate bootstrap, 0.5.71 shore-first lake/ocean freeze morphology, 0.5.69 cold-stress biomes/temperature diagnostics, 0.5.68 broken auroral curtains and 0.5.67 hydrology-shaped vegetation remain intact.
