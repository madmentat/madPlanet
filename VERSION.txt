VERSION 0.5.108
Base: 0.5.107
Changes: Keep the restored classic planet morphology and remove the suspiciously perfect circular outline from polar ice caps.
  - preserve the 0.5.107 simplex-based continent/island restoration, broad classic mountain morphology and post-Grok anti-artifact rendering foundation
  - polar display ice remains strictly opaque/binary: no feather, no translucent white fringe, no temporal alpha crossfade
  - remove the old dense-land shortcut that forced every physical land-ice sample above 70% to solid white and exposed a latitude-like circular isocontour
  - resolve almost the entire transitional polar cap against seamless 3-D geography; only a truly saturated physical ice core (>=99.5%) is guaranteed solid
  - make the edge field low-frequency dominant so polar caps can form broad bays, peninsulas and ice tongues, with smaller shoreline breakup layered on top
  - compute geographic edge noise through the full transitional land-ice range instead of skipping it above rawLand=0.82
  - retain the +15 C calibration that trims transitional polar-cap coverage by about ten percent without altering cryosphere mass or temperature
Regression: update the hard-edge cryosphere guard so future fixes cannot restore the 70% circular dense-cap shortcut or translucent ice.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
