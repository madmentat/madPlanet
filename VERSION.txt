VERSION 0.5.122
Base: 0.5.121
Changes: Add deterministic elliptical orbits, couple orbital distance to climate forcing, add a sun focus marker to the main-scene orbit HUD, and make the Thermal Imager scale draggable.
  - every world seed now resolves a reproducible moderate orbital eccentricity in the range e=0.010..0.180, biased toward near-circular terrestrial-style systems rather than comet-like extremes
  - the distance control remains the semi-major axis a; periapsis and apoapsis are derived as a(1-e) and a(1+e)
  - Kepler's equation is solved for eccentric anomaly and true anomaly so visual orbital position and star distance are no longer advanced as a uniform circular angle
  - Weather Core seasonal snapshots receive instantaneous stellar forcing proportional to 1/r^2; periapsis therefore heats more strongly than apoapsis and eccentricity can alter weather/climate evolution
  - the existing Kepler year period from semi-major axis and stellar mass remains unchanged
  - Orbit mini-map now draws a real ellipse with the star at one focus, current Kepler position, current radius r, eccentricity e and the existing axial/season diagnostics
  - main-scene Orbit HUD keeps the stable fixed screen-space scale from 0.5.121 but now uses the same eccentricity and Kepler position as the physical model
  - a compact schematic sun marker is drawn at the HUD ellipse focus after the orbit line, so the orbit visually passes behind the sun instead of through its centre
  - current planet remains pinned exactly to screen centre; camera zoom still cannot resize the navigation orbit
  - Thermal Imager legend can now be dragged with mouse or touch, is clamped to the viewport and remembers its position in localStorage
Regression: orbit-eccentricity.test.js validates deterministic e bounds, Kepler convergence, periapsis/apoapsis radii, stronger periapsis flux, Weather Core 1/r^2 forcing and shared physical eccentricity across both orbit displays. rubric-orbit-ui.test.js additionally guards the sun-focus occlusion and persistent thermal-legend dragging.
Snapshot: 0.5.121 remains the rollback point before eccentric orbital climate forcing and draggable thermal scale.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
