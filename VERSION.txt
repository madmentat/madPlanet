VERSION 0.5.131
Base: 0.5.130
Changes: Replace decorative river ownership with runoff-driven drainage physics tied to the Weather Core water cycle and resolved terrain.
  - close precipitation/infiltration/soil storage into a persistent effective-runoff field and route that water over the cubed-sphere macro terrain instead of inventing rivers from a shader noise zero-line
  - hydro-condition closed depressions with a Priority-Flood pass seeded from the resolved ocean, then build one acyclic downstream drainage edge per land cell and a topological contributing-area/discharge order
  - convert local runoff from kg/m²/s to physical m³/s using each cell area and planetary radius; accumulated discharge therefore grows downstream as real contributing basins merge
  - retain finite river memory/baseflow between individual precipitation ticks using soil/humidity-supported unresolved groundwater rather than a random perennial-river source
  - derive channel width, depth and velocity from discharge power laws in the hydraulic-geometry sense, and publish Q^m S^n stream-power potential for later geomorphic erosion work
  - derive lake support from hydro-conditioned depression depth plus available discharge, rather than allowing procedural FBM basins to decide whether a lake exists
  - rasterize the diagnosed cell-to-downstream graph into a denser temporally blended RGBA8 cubemap, producing continuous river corridors across Weather Core cells without square hydrology patches
  - make the surface shader consume that physical river/lake cubemap as the authoritative topology; the old FBM river/lake functions now only roughen edges and add sub-grid morphology inside physical corridors
  - let physical river/floodplain support feed riparian wetness and drought resistance, while local freeze/boil thermodynamics continue to decide whether inland water is liquid or frozen
  - keep drainage/discharge updates on the existing fixed Weather Core cadence; rendering only samples and blends a cubemap, so no CPU hydrology runs per frame
  - calibration/architecture follows DEM hydrology practice (conditioned elevation -> flow direction -> contributing runoff) and Leopold-Maddock hydraulic geometry rather than a latitude or decorative river mask
Regression: river-physics.test.js checks Priority-Flood pit escape, acyclic ocean drainage, downstream discharge growth, hydraulic-geometry monotonicity, stream-power response, dry-basin rejection, physical GPU graph rasterization and build/shader wiring. biome-state.test.js now requires physical river/lake corridors to own ecological floodplain wetness.
Snapshot: 0.5.130 remains the rollback point before runoff-driven visible drainage.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
