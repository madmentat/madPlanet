VERSION 0.5.139
Base: 0.5.138
Changes: Split visible rivers from the coarse Weather Core drainage lattice with basin-constrained fine tributaries.
  - keep Priority-Flood, runoff accumulation, discharge and the diagnosed physical downstream graph authoritative for water, basins and outlets
  - add river-visual-tributaries.js as a display-only secondary network: candidate headwaters require real humidity/soil water/runoff plus usable terrain slope
  - compute a physical basin id and graph distance to the nearest diagnosed channel/lake/ocean for every land cell
  - allow a visual tributary to choose among several neighbouring downhill cells only when they stay in the same physical basin and reduce distance to a diagnosed receiver; uphill and cross-divide steps are rejected
  - rebuild the visual tributary topology only every six Weather Core ticks or after a topology/seed change, keeping it off render FPS
  - rasterize tributaries as thin sub-cell curves whose lateral offsets collapse to zero at confluences, so small branches merge cleanly into the physical trunk instead of following cell centres
  - raise river display cubemap reconstruction from 7x to 8x Weather Core resolution while narrowing the physical trunk brush to reduce the broad blocky blue-groove look
  - preserve physical trunk/lake dominance at confluences by painting fine tributaries first and authoritative channels afterwards with max compositing
  - retain the 0.5.138 physical-fog spatial reconstruction and 0.5.137 single-writer integer T_a/T_f telemetry
Regression: add river-visual-tributaries.test.js for deterministic wetness/slope gating, basin locking, downhill routing, receiver convergence and dry-basin rejection; update river GPU expectations to model 5 / 8x reconstruction.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
