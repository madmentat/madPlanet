VERSION 0.5.126
Base: 0.5.125
Changes: Add an interactive thermal surface probe without adding GPU readback stalls.
  - thermal mode now exposes a thermometer-shaped cursor over the planet surface; the native canvas cursor is hidden only while a valid thermal hit exists
  - reproduce the render camera ray on the CPU, intersect the unit planet and rotate the hit with the same surface-body transform used by physicalFogSample
  - read the authoritative Weather Core surfaceSkinTemp field rather than inferring temperature from the rendered colour
  - bilinearly interpolate the cubed-sphere temperature field and mirror the thermal cubemap's five-direction 0.014 seam-smoothing footprint so the readout moves smoothly across coarse cell and cube-face boundaries
  - draw a leader from the measured point beyond the projected planet limb to a compact temperature callout; right-side hits place the callout to the right, left-side hits mirror it to the left
  - the callout and leader live on a pointer-transparent overlay above every application window, with a very high z-index and viewport clamping
  - include a small colour swatch generated from the same climate-focused thermal palette; the swatch mirrors to the outer edge of the left/right callout
  - keep the probe updated from the same scaled/paused synthetic render clock, so a stationary cursor follows the rotating surface correctly at x0/x1/etc simulation speeds
  - suppress the probe while the camera is actively dragged so it never steals pointer gestures; no readPixels, framebuffer or synchronous GPU operations are introduced
  - expose window.__madPlanetThermalProbe for lightweight diagnostics (visibility, current K value, palette and physical body sampling)
Regression: thermal-probe-ui.test.js guards camera handedness, physical surfaceSkinTemp sampling, body rotation, interpolation/seam smoothing, mirrored callout geometry, topmost overlay behaviour and the no-GPU-readback performance contract.
Snapshot: 0.5.125 remains the rollback point before the interactive thermal probe.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
