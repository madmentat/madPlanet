VERSION 0.5.60
Base: 0.5.59
Changes: Physical snow / land surface ice / sea ice. New js/cryosphere.js replaces the old instant latitude-temperature snowcaps with persistent Weather Core cryosphere state driven by landed snowfall, land-skin temperature, SST, latent heat and model time. surfaceSnowWater remains the snowfall reservoir; the old temperature-timescale precipMeltSurfaceSnow shortcut is retired and snow now melts by consuming sensible heat above 273.15 K, returning mass to surfaceLiquidWater. Cold landed liquid can freeze into persistent landIceWater, and deep cold snowpacks compact slowly into land ice rather than becoming glaciers in one tick. Persistent land ice is added to the mobile landed-H2O closure so conversion snow <-> ice <-> liquid does not create water. Sea ice grows only from sub-freezing seaSurfaceTemp near the seawater freezing point (271.35 K), with latent heat warming SST back toward the phase boundary; growth is rate-limited to ~0.14 m/day and melt to ~0.32 m/day so a cold first tick cannot paint a many-metre polar cap. Sea-ice concentration suppresses open-water evaporation. Snow/land ice/sea ice feed the existing clear-sky albedo path with a one-fixed-tick operator split; the older global climate iceArea remains only a slow one-box background used to recover non-ice albedo, not the local visual mask. New js/cryosphere-gpu.js publishes physical land-cryosphere and sea-ice coverage through one WebGL1-safe RGBA8 cubemap, storing previous/current endpoints in RG/BA and interpolating them with uCryosphereBlend without FPS-driven texture uploads. shaders/surface.glsl no longer contains snowN=temp+latitude or iceN=temp procedural cap generators: physical coverage controls where snow/ice exists, while noise only modulates surface texture. This removes giant instant polar caps on otherwise warm planets and lets seasonal snow, sea ice and persistent land ice grow/retreat with actual weather history.
Roadmap note: next stage is 0.5.61 broader volcanic gases / aerosols and their atmospheric-radiative coupling, unless cryosphere visual validation reveals a regression that needs a 0.5.60 hotfix first.
