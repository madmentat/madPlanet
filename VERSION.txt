VERSION 0.5.49
Base: 0.5.48
Changes: local Weather Core weather-front diagnosis v1 - resolved horizontal gradients of air temperature, relative humidity and pressure are evaluated on the existing cubed-sphere tangent stencil and combined with real wind convergence plus cross-front thermal advection. A meaningful thermal contrast is required, but a broad planetary temperature gradient alone cannot become a front without dynamical convergence/advection. Front type is diagnosed from -V·grad(T): cold advection -> cold front, warm advection -> warm front, near-zero advection under convergence -> stationary front. Each resolved front exposes strength, tangent/normal, convergence, thermal-advection and bounded frontal-ascent fields. Frontal ascent is consumed by the existing vertical-stability/cloud-layer model together with orographic lift, while the front layer itself does not modify temperature, pressure, vapor or condensate; mass/energy ownership remains with the existing physical modules. No procedural front morphology or GLSL visual coupling is introduced.
