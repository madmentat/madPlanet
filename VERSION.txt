VERSION 0.5.62
Base: 0.5.61
Changes: Lightning abundance and visibility repair. Physical lightning still requires a real deep moist convective column with a mixed-phase cloud layer, so clear/stable/shallow air cannot generate strikes. The old lightning diagnosis multiplied deep-state, updraft, condensate and mixed-phase gates, which made ordinary thunderstorms numerically almost invisible unless every proxy was extreme at once. 0.5.62 keeps mixed-phase depth as the hard physical requirement but combines the correlated plume-vigor indicators more softly, lowers the renderer selection floor, and slightly enlarges active storm footprints so ordinary cumulonimbus can populate the five physical lightning slots. shaders/main.glsl no longer calls lightningGlow only where procedural low-cloud alpha exceeds 0.08; that mask clipped bolt branches and could hide a complete physical strike. Lightning is now evaluated whenever a real CPU storm payload exists, while cloud alpha remains only a soft visibility factor. The storm controls are also made deliberately nonlinear near the top of their range: normal settings stay close to the previous appearance, while maximum flash-rate/activity/glow settings produce substantially more frequent and brighter electrical activity without inventing storms in clear sky.
Roadmap note: visually validate 0.5.62 before returning to the H2O-normalization stability work. Check an Earth-like wet world with ordinary convection at default lightning settings, then set storm activity/rate/glow to maximum and confirm that several active storm regions produce obvious repeated flashes without lightning appearing in physically clear regions.
