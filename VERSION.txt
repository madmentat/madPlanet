VERSION 0.5.117
Base: 0.5.116
Changes: Expand the second tool column with draggable Orbit-window behavior plus Ecliptic and Thermal Imager instrument modes.
  - Orbit remains the first tool in the new left column; Ecliptic and Thermal Imager occupy the next two default cells, leaving five of the sixteen logical toolbar slots empty
  - existing saved toolbar layouts migrate automatically: new tools take their default free cells or the first available empty cells without resetting the user's arrangement
  - Orbit window now has a compact title bar and close control and can be dragged by pointer/touch; its last position is saved locally and clamped back into the viewport after resize
  - Ecliptic mode draws a transparent projected instrument layer over the live planet: physical spin axis, equatorial great circle, ecliptic great circle and the current axial-obliquity angle follow the actual camera viewpoint
  - Thermal Imager recolors the planet disk from the existing Weather Core surface-temperature field stored in the shared fog/soil cubemap A channel (180..380 K), rather than adding a second temperature model or CPU upload
  - thermal palette includes fine 20 K isolines and a compact 180/280/380 K legend; clouds and ordinary lighting cannot hide the diagnostic surface temperature
  - Orbit, Ecliptic and Thermal modes are display-only state and never enter the world hash/seed
  - all three instrument modules are wrapped before runtime-settings so scaled simulation time and Pause remain coherent with the existing frame architecture
Regression: both bash and PowerShell builders include the new thermal shader and instrument modules in deterministic order; the existing sixteen-slot drag/swap toolbar contract remains intact.
Snapshot: 0.5.116 remains the rollback point before draggable Orbit and the Ecliptic/Thermal instruments.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
