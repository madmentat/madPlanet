VERSION 0.5.85
Base: 0.5.84
Changes: Removed the hard outer contour around tectonic influence zones. The remaining smooth dark/light bands were not the mountain ridges themselves: terrain relief was spatially switched on only when abs(belt.x)>0.004. That created a mathematically exact closed isoline around tectonic regions; finite-difference surface normals magnified the tiny height discontinuity, and the Tectonics slider amplified it further.
Fix: tectonic relief is now globally enabled whenever the Tectonics control is active, while the signed belt amplitude itself fades continuously to zero. Positive collision relief still scales with belt.x^2 and negative rift relief remains shallow, so both sides approach zero smoothly without a separate visible activation boundary.
Regression: tectonic-morphology.test.js now forbids reintroducing a fixed abs(belt.x)>0.004 spatial gate and requires the continuous signed-belt path.
Validation: confirm visible stamp v0.5.85. On a seed where the artefact is obvious, zoom close and sweep Tectonics from minimum to maximum. Mountain relief should strengthen, but a thin smooth contour surrounding the tectonic region should no longer appear or darken with the slider.
Retained: 0.5.84 tablet cadence/adaptive-resolution work and cheaper tectonic detail; 0.5.83 live FPS/T/star/AU HUD; 0.5.82 no grey Arctic lens; 0.5.81 opaque cryosphere phase logic; 0.5.80 gradual/irreversible volatile loss; 0.5.79 uncapped hot equilibrium; 0.5.78 phase/biology gating.
Snapshots: snapshot/0.5.84 remains the rollback point before this targeted contour fix. Create snapshot/0.5.85 only after the release head passes CI.
