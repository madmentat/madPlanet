VERSION 0.5.50
Base: 0.5.49
Changes: local Weather Core pressure-system diagnosis v1 - resolved local pressure extrema, seam-safe relative vorticity, hemisphere-aware cyclonic sign, wind convergence/divergence and frontal support now diagnose physical cyclones and anticyclones without random storm centres or procedural spiral tracks. Cyclones expose bounded ascent and anticyclones bounded subsidence; the existing vertical-stability/cloud-layer model consumes those motions so cyclones deepen cloud columns while anticyclonic subsidence stabilises and shallows them, without the pressure-system layer directly editing temperature, pressure, wind or H2O. The old seeded cycA/cycB synoptic weather path is functionally retired: immediately after every deriveWorld() both legacy arrays are replaced with all-zero compatibility buffers, so the historical shader vortex/front masks cannot affect morphology. Their inactive uniform/function ABI remains only until the planned 0.5.53 Weather Core cloud-visual rewrite, avoiding a risky standalone surgery on the large cloud shader before that stage.
Polish: parameter-panel toggles are fixed to one 30 px switch width and aligned to the right edge instead of inheriting the flexible .row label rule. The Random action is replaced in capture phase with a city-ready world generator: moderate rocky gravity/rotation, liquid-water inventory, mostly N2/O2 atmosphere with small H2/He variation, exactly zero CH4, low corrosive/greenhouse trace gases, K/G/F stars, and an orbital-distance solve against the current coupled climateModel(). Candidates are accepted only inside moderate temperature/pressure/O2/water/gravity and non-runaway climate envelopes; extreme worlds remain available through manual controls.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
