VERSION 0.5.43
Base: 0.5.42
Changes: local Weather Core H2O transport v1 - every cubed-sphere cell now owns a physical atmospheric vapor column in kg/m2 plus derived relative humidity and evaporation source. Warm liquid surfaces are identified from a CPU port of the same macro-continent field used by the shader moisture path, inject vapor according to local temperature, dryness and wind, and the 0.5.42 tangent wind field advects vapor conservatively across a precomputed cross-face finite-volume edge graph. Pure advection preserves area-weighted atmospheric H2O mass. Until 0.5.44 adds local saturation/condensation, the 0.5.37 one-box water budget remains authoritative for the global vapor reservoir, so each tick renormalizes only the spatial H2O field back to that conserved global amount instead of allowing unphysical vapor accumulation.
Release rule: every new archive increments patch version by 0.0.1 and is named madPlanet-X.Y.Z.zip.
