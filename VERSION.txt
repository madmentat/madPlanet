VERSION 0.5.47
Base: 0.5.46
Changes: local Weather Core soil hydrology v1 - landed liquid water now infiltrates a finite terrain-dependent soil-moisture reservoir, saturated/ponded excess becomes runoff, and runoff is routed conservatively toward the lowest resolved macro-terrain neighbour on the existing cubed-sphere stencil. Flat land holds deeper soil while rugged tectonic belts have shallower capacity and faster surface runoff; frozen ground suppresses infiltration. Moist soil can evaporate back to atmospheric vapor, and slow drainage above field capacity feeds runoff. Closed depressions retain unresolved runoff for the later river/lake closure, while runoff reaching the macro-ocean explicitly returns to the bulk condensed reservoir. The mobile weather-scale H2O closure now includes atmosphere, landed liquid/snow, soil moisture and unresolved runoff, so infiltration and drainage repartition water without creating or deleting it.
